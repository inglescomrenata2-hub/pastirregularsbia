📌 Lembrete rápido

Regulares: verbo + -ed → play → played · watch → watched · start → started · marry → married

Irregulares: mudam a forma (é preciso decorar!)

Negativa: didn't + verbo normal → She didn't play Eleven.  ·  Pergunta: Did + pessoa + verbo normal → Did she play Eleven?
